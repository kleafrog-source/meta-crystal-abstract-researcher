# <img src="../assets/readme-icons/service-bay.svg" alt="" width="96" height="96" align="absmiddle" /> Service Bay

Index status and manual maintenance at [`/service-bay`](http://localhost:3000/service-bay).

- Inspect text-index and derived-anchor coverage for configured encoders.
- Build or rebuild CLAP and SigLIP2 indexes and supported catalogue-derived indexes.
- Review stale/missing states and job progress; use explicit refresh and stop controls where available.
- Work modes consume indexes; Service Bay owns their manual rebuild controls.

See the [index matrix](../service-bay-index-matrix.md).
